01 / A quiet night

Poem Quest

静夜思 · 李白

Li Bai sits alone in a guest room. Moonlight enters through the window.

BEGIN EXPERIENCE

02 / Moonlight

月光似霜

A pale patch of light lies beside the bed. Frost?

EXPLORE THE MOONLIGHT

The floor is still. It is moonlight, not frost.

床前明月光，
疑是地上霜。

FOLLOW THE LIGHT UPWARD

03 / Look up

举头望月

The light leads from the floor to the window.

RAISE YOUR GAZE

Li Bai lifts his head toward the moon. For a moment, home feels close.

举头望明月。

FOLLOW THE MOON HOME

04 / Family memory

家人团聚

A lantern above the family table. An elder pours tea; a child reaches for a bowl.

STAY AT THE TABLE

Someone draws an empty chair closer. For a moment, there is a place for him.

Imagined memory of home; not a literal event stated in the poem.

STEP INTO THE COURTYARD

05 / Friends memory

朋友相聚

Outside, beneath a pavilion, friends pass a cup of wine and trade lines of poetry.

LISTEN A MOMENT

A familiar voice begins a verse. He remembers how the others would finish it.

Imagined memory of home; not a literal event stated in the poem.

LET THE MEMORY FADE

06 / Return and reflection

低头思故乡

The voices are gone. Alone in the cold guest room again, Li Bai lowers his head toward the moonlit floor.

床前明月光，
疑是地上霜。
举头望明月，
低头思故乡。
